## On writing slides as prose

A deck read on a phone, days after the talk, is an essay with pictures. Folio sets it as one: a column kept to reading measure, a drop cap to start, and the margin left for a note.

The rest of the slide stays empty on purpose. White space is not waste; it is where the eye rests between ideas.

::aside::

The `essay` layout. An `::aside::` slot lands in the margin, set small and italic.
