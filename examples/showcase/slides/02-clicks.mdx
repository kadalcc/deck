# Clicks

A slide can say one thing at a time. Each press reveals the next step; going back undoes it.

<Notes>
Chapter two: steps inside a slide. reveal.js calls them fragments, Slidev calls them clicks; here both spellings work.
</Notes>

## Saying exactly when

<div className="sc-timeline">
  <div><code>{"at={1}"}</code><Click at={1} className="sc-pill">from step 1</Click></div>
  <div><code>{'at="+0"'}</code><Click at="+0" className="sc-pill">with the one before</Click></div>
  <div><code>{'at="+2"'}</code><Click at="+2" className="sc-pill">two steps after it</Click></div>
  <div><code>{"hide at={2}"}</code><Click hide at={2} className="sc-pill is-muted">here until step 2</Click></div>
  <div><code>{"at={[2, 4]}"}</code><Click at={[2, 4]} className="sc-pill">steps 2 and 3, then gone</Click></div>
</div>

<p className="sc-small"><code>clicks: 5</code> in the frontmatter adds a last step that reveals nothing — a beat to talk over. <StepMeter /></p>

<Notes>
Positions are absolute — `at={1}` — or relative to the element before: `+0` shares its step, `+2` lands two after it. A pair is a range.
[click] Step one: the absolute pill, and the `+0` with it.
[click] Step two: `hide` takes its pill away, and the range arrives.
[click] Step three: `+2`, counted from step one.
[click] Step four: the range is over.
[click] And step five exists only because `clicks: 5` in frontmatter says so — a pause before the slide moves on.
</Notes>

## Forward and back <DirectionBadge />

<div className="sc-timeline">
  <div><code>forward:delay-300</code><Click className="sc-pill forward:delay-300">waits a beat, going forward only</Click></div>
  <div><code>backward:duration-0</code><Click effect="fade-up" className="sc-pill backward:duration-0">rises in, and snaps back out</Click></div>
  <div><code>deck-instant-back</code><Click effect="grow" className="sc-pill deck-instant-back">no Tailwind: the plain class</Click></div>
</div>

<p className="sc-small">The deck root carries <code>data-nav-direction</code>; the starter's Tailwind has <code>@custom-variant forward</code> and <code>backward</code> (Slidev's variants), and <code>--deck-nav-sign</code> is 1 or −1 for your own CSS. <StepMeter /></p>

<Notes>
Every move has a direction, and styles can read it.
[click] Going forward this pill waits three hundred milliseconds — `forward:delay-300` — and going back it leaves at once.
[click] This one rises in with its fade, and `backward:duration-0` takes it away instantly when you step back.
[click:3] Without Tailwind, `deck-instant-back` does the same for any element. Step back through the slide to see the difference; the badge in the title says which way the deck last moved.
</Notes>
