<Notes>

<Notes>
One build, many pages. The scroll view is for reading on a phone; print lays out every slide — and every step, with `pdfSeparateFragments` — as a page. The presenter, audience, notes and stats pages are routes of the same bundle. A projector window takes no input and always follows. And any slide has an address: by position, `#/5/2/1` for column five, row two, step one — or by its `id`, like this one.
</Notes>

## A slide with options of its own

<Cols n={2} gap="2rem" align="start">
<Col>

This slide is `zoom: 0.8` — everything on it is set smaller, for a slide with more to show.

It carries `state: calm`, which puts a `state-calm` class on it for your CSS. And the slide after it in the source is `hide: true`: built, but never shown.

</Col>
<Col>

<How>

```yaml
---
zoom: 0.8
state: calm
hideInToc: true
---
```

```yaml
---
hide: true      # built, never shown
---
```

</How>

</Col>
</Cols>

<Notes>
Per-slide options: `zoom` scales a slide's content, `state` adds a class, `hideInToc` keeps it out of the table of contents, and `hide` leaves a slide in the source but out of the deck.
</Notes>

## Export

<Cols cols="1fr 1fr" gap="3rem" align="start">
<Col>

<How label="in the deck's folder">

```sh
kadal-deck export                  # PDF
kadal-deck export --png --pptx     # + a PNG per page, PowerPoint
kadal-deck export --scale 1 --out handouts
```

</How>

</Col>
<Col>

A headless browser prints `?print-pdf`: each PDF page is a picture of the slide with its text over it, so glass, glows and gradients look the same in every viewer and the words can still be searched. The PowerPoint is a picture per slide with your notes attached — it opens in Keynote and Google Slides looking exactly like the web.

<Callout kind="tip">Embedded pages are photographed first, so paper gets a picture where the page was.</Callout>

</Col>
</Cols>

<Notes>
Export drives a headless Chromium over the print view. PDF comes from the browser's own printer; PNGs are a screenshot of each page; the PPTX wraps those pictures one per slide with the speaker notes attached.
</Notes>
