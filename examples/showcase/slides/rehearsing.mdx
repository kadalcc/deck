## The deck reads itself aloud

<Cols cols="1fr 1fr" gap="3rem" align="start">
<Col>

<Clicks>

- <Kbd>R</Kbd> (or <code>?narrate</code>) opens the narration bar: the notes are read aloud, each <code>[click]</code> part at its step, and the pages turn by themselves
- The timeline has a point for every chapter — every <code>layout: section</code> slide — and a dot for every slide; hover for a picture of it
- Three voices: clips baked by <code>kadal-deck narrate</code>, Kokoro running in the browser, or the browser's own speech

</Clicks>

</Col>
<Col>

<How>

```yaml
---

---
```

```yaml
---

---
```

</How>

<Notes>
Press S to open the presenter window; it stays in step with this one through the browser, and with the room when there is one.
[click] The notes are split at each `[click]` marker, and the presenter view lights the part for the current step — this sentence is lit at step two.
