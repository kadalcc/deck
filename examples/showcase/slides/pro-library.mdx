How to use it, and where it works. A `pro/` name in `layout:` or `background:` is all it takes. On deck.kadal.cc, a deck published from a Pro or Team workspace gets the real item. Anywhere else — your own host, a static build, the free plan — the name falls back to the theme's background and the default layout: the slide still reads, nothing errors, and only the author sees a chip saying the item is Pro. The blobs behind this slide are `pro/blob-morph`, from the startup genre.
