## Pictures

`image:` in frontmatter fills half the slide. Inside a slide, `<Image>` is lazy, and `preview` opens it in a lightbox — click one:

<div className="sc-thumbs">
  <Image src="media/sea-thumb.jpg" preview alt="The sea at dusk — click to open" />
  <Image src="media/night-thumb.jpg" preview alt="The sea at night — click to open" />
</div>

<p className="sc-small"><Kbd>Esc</Kbd> closes the lightbox.</p>

<Notes>
`image-right`, the mirror of image-left. The two thumbnails are `<Image preview>`: click one and it opens full size over the deck; Escape closes it. The F key labels them too, so the keyboard can open them.
</Notes>

## Other pages

The page on the right is live: click it, then press its button. <Kbd>F</Kbd> labels it for the keyboard too.

It loads when the slide is shown and unloads when you leave; in print and the overview, its `poster:` stands in.

<Notes>
`iframe-right` with `url:`. The shield keeps your keys driving the deck until you click the page; Done hands them back. A Reload button is always there, for a page that failed to load in the middle of a talk.
</Notes>
