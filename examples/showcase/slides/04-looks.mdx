<p className="sc-small">Press <Kbd>T</Kbd> to wear one, <Kbd>D</Kbd> to flip light and dark. Each screen remembers its own choice. A theme is a class of CSS tokens — colours, type, radius, padding — so writing your own is one stylesheet.</p>

<Notes>
minimal is the default — black on white, one accent. rla is the dark, dot-matrix look with an accessible body face. stack is warm neutrals with an emerald accent. Each has a light and a dark scheme.

These cards are the real themes: each wears the theme's class, so its tokens apply inside it. Press T for the menu — the choice is per screen and remembered per deck, so the projector and your laptop can differ.
</Notes>

## A dark slide in a light deck

`colorScheme: dark` in one slide's frontmatter gives it the dark tokens, whatever the deck is wearing — for a background that needs light text.

<How>

```yaml
---
colorScheme: dark
background:
  component: glows
  colors: "#0ea5e9,#6366f1,#14b8a6"
---
```

</How>

<Notes>
Per-slide colour scheme. The deck may be light, but this slide is always dark, because its background asks for it. The reverse — `colorScheme: light` — works too.
</Notes>

## A colour

Any CSS colour: a hex, `rgb()`, `oklch()`, or a name.

<Notes>
The plainest background: a colour, as a string. Named colours work too — `background: navy`.
</Notes>

## A component, with a wash

Props go beside `component`. With a component, `color` is its stroke and `fill` — or `tint` — washes the layer behind it in one colour, strongest top-left.

<How>

```yaml
background:
  component: topo
  color: "rgba(5,150,105,0.55)"
  fill: "rgba(5,150,105,0.14)"
```

</How>

<Notes>
Contour lines in emerald over an emerald wash. `fill` is never a flat block: a diagonal gradient of the one colour.
</Notes>

----
background:
  tint: "rgba(236,72,153,0.16)"
id: bg-tint
---

<p className="sc-eyebrow">background: {"{ tint }"}</p>

## A wash on its own

`tint:` without a component: one colour, as a soft diagonal gradient instead of a flat fill.

<Notes>
The same wash, alone. Plain `color:` stays flat; `tint:` gives the gradient.
</Notes>

## Props on any background

<Background name="constellation" opacity={0.6} />

Behind this text: a starfield of 500, from frontmatter, and a constellation layered inside the slide with `<Background name="constellation" />`.

<Notes>
Two layers: the slide's own background from frontmatter, and a second one placed inside the slide with the `<Background>` component — any registered background, at any opacity.
</Notes>
