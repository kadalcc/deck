## Any icon, by name

<Cols cols="1fr 1fr" gap="2.5rem" align="start">
<Col>

<p className="sc-icons-big"><mdi-github /> <logos-figma /> <logos-docker-icon /> <logos-cloudflare-icon /> <logos-python /></p>

The tag form or `<Icon name>`: every Iconify set, and Lucide names still work bare. Sized and coloured like text.

<p className="sc-small">A built deck carries the drawings from its own <code>@iconify-json/*</code> packages; the hosted builder fetches them once from the Iconify API. <code>kadal-deck check</code> names a misspelt icon and the set to install.</p>

</Col>
<Col>

<How>

```mdx
<mdi-github /> <logos-figma />

<mdi-weather-night class="text-sky-500" />
<mdi-heart style="color: #e11d48" />

<Icon name="mdi:compass-rose" color="var(--deck-accent)" />
<Icon name="anchor" />
```

```sh
bun add -d @iconify-json/logos @iconify-json/mdi
```

</How>

</Col>
</Cols>

<Notes>
Icons from any Iconify set. The compiler turns `<mdi-heart />` into `<Icon name="mdi:heart" />`. Class and style size and colour them like text. Lucide icons by their bare names keep working, and need no package.
</Notes>

## How a mark is drawn

<Cols cols="1.15fr 1fr" gap="2.5rem" align="start">
<Col className="sc-words">

<ul className="sc-mark-list">
  <li>A <Mark type="underline" at={1} strokeWidth={8} iterations={1}>thick single stroke</Mark></li>
  <li>A <Mark type="circle" at={2} padding={14} duration={2000}>slow, roomy circle</Mark></li>
  <li>A <Mark type="box" at={3} animate={false} color="#0ea5e9">box, not drawn</Mark></li>
  <li>A <Mark type="highlight" at={4} opacity={0.6} delay={500} color="#a855f7">late, strong highlight</Mark></li>
  <li>And <Mark at={0} type="underline" color="#10b981">one always there</Mark></li>
</ul>

</Col>
<Col>

<How>

```mdx
<Mark strokeWidth={8} iterations={1}>…</Mark>
<Mark type="circle" padding={14} duration={2000}>…</Mark>
<Mark type="box" animate={false}>…</Mark>
<Mark type="highlight" opacity={0.6} delay={500}>…</Mark>
<Mark at={0}>…</Mark>
```

</How>

</Col>
</Cols>

<Notes>
A mark's options. The green underline has `at={0}`: it is there from the start and takes no step.
[click] Eight pixels wide, one stroke instead of two.
[click] Fourteen pixels of room, drawn over two seconds.
[click] No animation: the box is simply there.
[click] Half a second's wait, then a highlight at sixty percent.
</Notes>

## Drag it where it goes

<Cols cols="1fr 1fr" gap="2.5rem" align="start">
<Col>

In `kadal-deck dev`, **double-click** a placed element: drag it, pull a corner or an edge (Shift keeps the shape), turn it by the round handle, nudge it with the arrow keys. The new position is written into deck.mdx a moment later — and nothing else in the file changes.

<How>

```mdx
---
dragPos:
  badge: 1300,170,480,_
---

<Drag pos="badge">…</Drag>
<Drag pos="1180,600,460,_,-4">…</Drag>
<DragArrow pos="1060,520,330,-200" />
```

</How>

</Col>
<Col />
</Cols>

<Drag pos="badge">
<Glass glow>
<p className="sc-drag-card"><logos-cloudflare-icon /> Placed by name, from the frontmatter</p>
</Glass>
</Drag>

<Drag pos="1180,600,460,_,-4">
<Callout kind="tip" title="Drag me">Positions are stage pixels: x, y, width, height (`_`: as tall as it needs) and a turn.</Callout>
</Drag>

<DragArrow pos="1060,520,330,-200" color="var(--deck-accent)" width={4} />

<Notes>
Elements placed by hand. The card at the top right takes its position from this slide's frontmatter by name; the tip below it carries its own. In kadal-deck dev, double-click either to move, resize or turn it; the arrow's ends drag the same way. Elsewhere — a build, the hosted copy, a PDF — they are simply placed.
</Notes>
