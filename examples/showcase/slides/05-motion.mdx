## Auto-animate

<div className="sc-aa-row">
  <div data-id="tide-box" className="sc-aa-box">high</div>
  <div data-id="tide-dot" className="sc-aa-dot" />
</div>

Elements with the same `data-id` on two neighbouring `autoAnimate: true` slides move between their places. Press <Kbd>↓</Kbd>.

<Notes>
Auto-animate, reveal.js's FLIP: two slides side by side or one above the other, both `autoAnimate: true`, and every element sharing a `data-id` animates from its old box to its new one — position, size, colour and radius.
</Notes>

## This slide moves on by itself

<p className="sc-countdown"><Timer seconds={6} /></p>

`autoSlide: 6000` in its frontmatter: six seconds, then the next slide. <Kbd>A</Kbd> pauses and resumes; a click anywhere stops it.

<Notes>
Auto-slide. A deck can set `autoSlide` for every slide — a kiosk, a loop in a foyer — or a single slide can, like this one: six seconds and it moves on. A pauses it; any click stops it. On a deck that sets nothing, A still starts a gentle five-second advance.
</Notes>
