# The live room

Phones join by QR. They follow along, react, ask, vote and play — nothing to install.

<p><LiveStatus /></p>

<Notes>
Chapter thirteen: the room. Served by a host — `kadal-deck publish`, or your own Worker — the deck gets a room: one Durable Object per deck that every window and phone connects to. Without one, as in a local preview, every piece in this chapter still renders and works for a single screen; the badge above says which this copy is.
