The journey the tour walks, in four stages. Build it: write the deck in MDX, with code, themes, backgrounds and motion. Rehearse it: let the deck read your notes aloud and pace you with a timer. Present it: the presenter view, your phone as the clicker, a pen and a laser. Own the room: the audience joins from their phones and votes, answers and asks.

Every link on a card goes to the chapter or slide that shows it — `<Link to="poll">` jumps by a slide's id. The cards are plain HTML in the MDX, styled in this deck's own stylesheet.
</Notes>
