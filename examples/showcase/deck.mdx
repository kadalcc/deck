<Notes>
Welcome. This is the engine's own tour: every feature it has appears on a slide, and every slide says how it was written.

The deck is wide and deep — moving right follows the chapters, moving down opens the variations under a slide. Press ? at any time for the keys, S for the presenter view you would use on stage.
</Notes>

<Notes>
That is the whole engine. The confetti is `<Confetti />`, fired when the slide arrives.

`npm create @kadal/deck` scaffolds a deck like this one: deck.mdx, deck.config.ts, and a Vite config — `kadal-deck dev` to write, `kadal-deck build` for any static host, `kadal-deck export` for PDF, PNG and PowerPoint.
