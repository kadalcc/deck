A teleprompter for the presenter view. The new **Teleprompter** button (or Shift T, or the `present.teleprompter` command) opens your speaker notes alone, big, in a window of their own that follows your slide and click step. In Chrome and Edge the button lists your screens by name and size, opens the teleprompter filling the one you pick, and remembers it for next time; in other browsers you get a window to drag where you want it. The current part of the notes sits on a reading line a third of the way down, earlier parts are dimmed, and the next slide's first lines wait underneath. You can change the text size and line width, mirror it for a prompter's glass, show a reading guide, let it scroll by itself (Space to start and stop, the arrow keys for speed), switch light or dark, and go fullscreen. A strip along the top shows the slide, your timer with its pacing, and, with a live room, raised hands, the pace votes, viewers and open questions. It does not count as a viewer, and its keys never move the deck. The presenter view keeps its own notes.

The presenter view's timer is now two compact rows: the time with its paused or over tag, then play/pause, reset, settings, the pacing badge and the clock. Long deck titles stop at two lines, with the whole title on hover.
