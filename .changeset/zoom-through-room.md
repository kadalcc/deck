Zoom reaches the projector on a hosted deck. The presenter's zoom (Alt+click, or the phone remote's Zoom) now travels through the room like the spotlight. The projector window and every audience screen that follows the presenter zoom with it, and a screen browsing on its own keeps its own view. Before, it only zoomed the presenter view.
