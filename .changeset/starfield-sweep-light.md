`starfield` and `sweep` read on a light page. On a light slide the starfield draws ink-coloured stars whose trails fade into the page, instead of painting the slide black under a dark title; the sweep's default palette becomes pale blues and teal. Dark slides look as before, and colours you set yourself are used as given in both schemes. Every component background now also receives the slide's `scheme` ("light" or "dark").
