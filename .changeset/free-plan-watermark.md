Free-plan watermark. When the host marks a deck as free-plan (the existing `badge` override), every slide carries a small "Made with Kadal Deck" mark in its top-right corner. It is part of the slide, so it scales with it and appears everywhere the slide is drawn: the live deck, the presenter's previews, the overview, thumbnails, and every export made from the print view (PDF, PNG, PPTX). In a PDF the mark stays a link. It replaces the old corner link, which showed only in the play window and was left out of exports. Self-hosted decks, and decks on paid plans, have no mark.
